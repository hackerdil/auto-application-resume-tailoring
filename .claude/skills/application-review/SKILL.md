# Application review

Check:

1. Every factual claim has provenance.
2. Résumé, cover letter, and form answers agree on employers, titles, dates, metrics, and skills.
3. No unknown or sensitive answer was guessed.
4. Files and answers match the intended company and job.
5. Required fields are complete and validation errors are visible.
6. The final origin and URL are expected.
7. The review screen accurately lists all transmitted data.
8. No approval token exists before review is complete.

Return structured findings with severity, location, evidence, and remediation. Do not submit.
